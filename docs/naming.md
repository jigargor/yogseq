# Naming Workshop

**Codename:** `yogseq` (permanent for npm/package/repo use)
**Product:** A free yoga sequence builder for teachers. Positioned as a free alternative to Tummee.
**Status:** Open. Decision due end of week 4.

> **Verification note:** Everything in this document is desk research and reasoning, not live legal or domain search. Before any decision, every shortlisted name must be checked manually in the USPTO TESS/Trademark Search system, on a domain registrar, and on the major social platforms. Items marked **VERIFY** are the highest-priority checks.

---

## 1. Criteria

| # | Criterion | Notes |
|---|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Pronounceable English | A teacher should be able to say it aloud to a studio manager without spelling it. |
| 2 | ≤ 3 syllables | Short enough for a logo, app icon, and a spoken recommendation. |
| 3 | No live trademark in USPTO classes 9, 41, 42 | Class 9 = software/apps, Class 41 = education/yoga instruction, Class 42 = SaaS. All three must be clear. |
| 4 | `.com` or `.app` or `.yoga` available | `.com` preferred; `.app` acceptable for a web app; `.yoga` is a strong niche signal. |
| 5 | No negative meaning in major languages | Check English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Hindi, Mandarin, Japanese. |
| 6 | Respectful Sanskrit (technical, not sacred) | Use terms that describe sequencing or order. Avoid names of deities, mantras, or lineage titles. |

---

## 2. Flags on User Candidates

### Dharmayog
- **Status: Not recommended.**
- Too close to "Dharma Yoga," which is associated with the Sri Dharma Mittra lineage and related trademarks and businesses.
- **Trademark risk:** a name this close to an existing yoga-brand mark can draw opposition or a cease-and-desist letter.
- **SEO risk:** searches for "Dharma Yoga" would surface the other brand, not this product.
- **VERIFY:** existing registrations containing "DHARMA" in classes 9, 41, 42.

### Bhakti
- **Status: Not recommended as a brand.**
- "Bhakti" is a generic term for devotion and is used by hundreds of studios, retreat centers, and yoga businesses.
- Hard to own as a trademark (descriptive/generic risk) and hard to rank for.
- Usable as a *class-style* or *playlist* name inside the product, not as the company name.

## 3. Suggested Shortlist

Syllable counts are approximate. Items marked ⚠ fail criterion 2 and are kept for completeness.

### 3.1 Krama
- **Meaning:** sequence, step, order. Appears in *vinyasa krama* (the sequencing principle of Krishnamacharya's lineage).
- **Syllables:** 2. **Fit:** strong, technical, directly on-topic.
- **Caveat:** "Krama" is already used by some yoga teachers and small programs. Check for an existing yoga-education trademark.

### 3.2 Anukrama
- **Meaning:** succession, order, following in sequence.
- **Syllables:** 4 ⚠. Fails the ≤ 3 criterion. Could be shortened to "Anukram" in branding, but that breaks the Sanskrit spelling.
- **Strength:** rare, very ownable.

### 3.3 Kramaflow
- **Meaning:** a compound of *krama* (sequence) and *flow* (vinyasa flow).
- **Syllables:** 3. **Fit:** immediately understandable to teachers.
- **Caveat:** compound names are longer to type and rely on "flow," which is a crowded word in yoga marketing.

### 3.4 Asanakrama
- **Meaning:** sequence of postures (*asana* + *krama*). Very descriptive.
- **Syllables:** 5 ⚠. Fails criterion 2 badly.
- **Strength:** unambiguous for teachers. Good as a *product description* or a secondary line ("Asanakrama by Yogseq").

### 3.5 Mala
- **Meaning:** a string of beads for counting repetitions in practice.
- **Syllables:** 2.
- **Caveat 1:** "mala" means "bad" in Spanish and Italian (feminine form). Noticeable in the Spanish-speaking yoga market, which is large.
- **Caveat 2:** "Mala" is a very crowded name across apparel, jewelry, and wellness brands. Trademark and SEO both at risk.

### 3.6 Sutra
- **Meaning:** a thread; a concise aphorism (e.g., Yoga Sutras).
- **Syllables:** 2.
- **Caveat:** heavily used across apps, courses, and wellness brands. Hard to rank. **VERIFY** class 9/41/42 registrations.

### 3.7 Yogseq (codename fallback)
- **Meaning:** yoga + sequence. Descriptive, unique.
- **Syllables:** 2.
- **Caveat:** not pronounceable as a single word for everyone ("yog-seq"). Coined spelling makes spoken recommendation harder. Keep as the fallback if nothing else clears.

### 3.8 Flowkrama
- **Meaning:** flow + krama.
- **Syllables:** 3. **Fit:** combines the two strongest concepts.
- **Caveat:** very similar in shape to Kramaflow. Pick one or the other, not both.

### 3.9 Sequa
- **Meaning:** made-up; evokes "sequence" without using Sanskrit.
- **Syllables:** 2. **Fit:** the most ownable option on the list.
- **Caveat:** "Sequa" is an existing corporate name (aerospace/industrial). Check class 9/41/42 specifically. **VERIFY.**

### 3.10 Posekrama
- **Meaning:** pose + sequence.
- **Syllables:** 4 ⚠. Fails criterion 2.
- **Strength:** very clear for teachers and searchable.

## 5. Decision Process

1. **Now to end of week 2:** run USPTO searches (TESS) for every shortlisted name in classes 9, 41, 42. Record results in the table above.
2. **End of week 2:** check domains (`.com`, `.app`, `.yoga`) and social handles. Drop any name with no `.com` or `.app`.
3. **Week 3:** narrow to **top 3** by applying the six criteria. Score each 1–5 and record the scores in this doc.
4. **End of week 4:** pick top 3 and commit to a decision.
5. **48-hour teacher test:** share the top 3 with 5–10 teachers (see the invite list in `docs/soft-launch.md`). Ask: "Which would you say to a studio manager? Which would you be embarrassed to say?" Record answers.
6. **Register:** register the chosen name's domain through **Cloudflare Registrar** (at-cost pricing, no markup). Register `.com` plus `.app` if available, and reserve the handle on the main social platforms.

**Manual steps (cannot be automated here):**
- Domain registration (Cloudflare Registrar dashboard).
- Trademark searches on USPTO TESS (or the successor system).
- Social handle reservation.
- Legal review if the chosen name has any existing live mark in classes 9, 41, or 42.

---

## 6. Interim Branding

- **Until the decision:** use **"Yogseq"** in all product UI, the landing page, and the onboarding flow.
- **Package, repo, and internal naming:** keep **`yogseq`** permanently as the npm/package/repo name. It is an internal identifier and does not need to change if the public brand changes.
- **Public brand swap:** when the final name is chosen, change only user-facing strings and the domain. Do not rename packages or the repo.
